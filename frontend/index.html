How to Play:

The Basics:

Each round, everyone will be provided a board of words following a predetermined theme. One word on the board is designated as the Target Word, visible to all player not designated as Morp, who sees the board but not which word is safe.

Taking Turns:

Safe players draw something that hints at the target word without giving it away. You want other safe players to know you see the word, but not make it so obvious the Morp can just copy you.

The Morp has to wing it. Watch the other drawings, pick up on any patterns, and try to blend in. If you go first, you get to see half the board to help narrow it down.

Voting:

After everyone draws, players vote on who they think the Morp is. The player with the most votes gets accused and roles are revealed.

  • Wrong accusation: The Morp deals 1 damage to a player of their choosing.
  • Morp caught: They get one shot at guessing the target word. Miss it and they take 1 damage instead.

Winning:

Players start with 3 health. Last one standing wins. When only 2 players remain, the game shifts into a special head-to-head mode. Both players are designated as morp and must race to guess the word while the eliminated players provide hints about the target word.

Tip:

You aren't limited to just drawing pictures, there are no rules against drawing words do whatever you want as long as it conveys some meaningful information.
